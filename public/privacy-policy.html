Privacy Policy

Your trust is our priority. Learn how SS Prime Infra collects, protects, and respects your personal information.

Effective Date: July 2026 | Last Updated: July 2026

1 Introduction

Welcome to SS Prime Infra ("Company", "we", "our", or "us"). We operate real estate consulting services across Gurgaon, Pune, and surrounding regions. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices when you visit our website (ssprimeinfra.com) or submit property enquiries, callback requests, or job applications through our platform.

2 Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when interacting with our property advisors or website forms:

  • Personal Contact Data: Full Name, Email Address, Mobile/Phone Number.
  • Property Preferences: Interested City (Gurgaon, Pune), preferred developer projects, budget range, and property configuration (2 BHK, 3 BHK, Luxury Villa, Commercial Space).
  • Career Submissions: Resumes/CVs, cover letters, and contact details submitted via our Careers portal.
  • Technical & Usage Log: IP address, browser type, device information, and page interaction metrics collected standardly via cookies and server logs.

3 How We Use Your Information

Your information is utilized strictly to provide tailored real estate services and consultation:

Consultation & Callback

Connecting you with our property specialists for verified project details, floor plans, and pricing.

Site Visit Scheduling

Arranging guided project site tours and developer appointments in Gurgaon and Pune.

Project Updates

Sending launch updates, price sheets, and special payment schemes for your requested projects.

Service Improvement

Enhancing website functionality, responsiveness, and customer assistance channels.

4 Data Security & No Third-Party Sales

We enforce strict administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

Strict Confidentiality Guarantee

SS Prime Infra does NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal contact information to third-party marketing companies, spam telemarketers, or unauthorized data brokers.

5 Cookies & Map Embeds

Our website utilizes standard browser cookies to optimize navigation and remember user preferences. Interactive features such as embedded Google Maps (for our Gurgaon & Pune offices) may store functional cookies subject to Google's standard privacy policies.

6 Your Rights & Opt-Out

You maintain complete control over your data. You may at any time:

  • Request correction or updating of your personal details.
  • Unsubscribe or opt-out from property updates via Email, WhatsApp, or Phone calls.
  • Request complete erasure of your submitted details from our active records.
